LIFEPASS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

LIFEPASS is a digital product passport service. This policy explains the information handled by the LIFEPASS website and mobile application.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and profile information such as the name you provide.
  • Product passport information: product name, type, brand, model, serial number, purchase date, warranty date, price, condition, owner details, notes, service history and related passport information.
  • Files and images: product images and documents you choose to add. The current browser implementation stores passport records and uploaded document data on your device.
  • Verification information: information submitted when you request LIFEPASS verification.
  • AI requests: messages and relevant product context you submit when using LIFEPASS AI.

How we use information

  • To create and maintain LIFEPASS accounts.
  • To create and display digital product passports and related history.
  • To process verification requests and maintain verification status.
  • To provide marketplace, transfer, QR/public-passport and product-analysis features you choose to use.
  • To provide LIFEPASS AI when you request it.
  • To protect the service, troubleshoot problems and maintain security.

Where information is stored

LIFEPASS uses Supabase for account authentication and certain verification/service functions. Passport records, profile images and browser-managed product documents may also be stored locally in your browser/device. Public passport links can expose product information intentionally included in the public passport; private owner information is designed not to be included in public QR passport data.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate LIFEPASS, such as authentication, database and AI infrastructure providers. Information can also be shared when you explicitly publish a public passport, create a marketplace listing, request verification, or otherwise use a feature that requires disclosure.

Security

LIFEPASS uses account authentication and security controls provided by its service infrastructure. Do not upload passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents to a product passport.

Your choices and deletion

You can stop using LIFEPASS and remove browser-stored passport data from the device/browser where it is stored. For account or service-side data requests, contact lifepass.digital@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children

LIFEPASS is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

We may update this policy as LIFEPASS changes. The latest version remains available on this page.
